GTA VI-Inspired HUD - GTA VI stars for Los Santos RED
====================================================

Los Santos RED (LSR) keeps GTA's own wanted level at zero and draws stars of its own, so with LSR the HUD's stars
don't come up. These are LSR's 4 star pictures redrawn with the HUD's GTA VI stars, at LSR's size (64 x 64):

  wantedstarwhite.png       a lit star                  (the HUD's white star)
  wantedstarred.png         a lit star, high levels     (the HUD's red star)
  wantedstargreyed.png      an unlit star               (the HUD's grey star)
  wantedstarwhitegrey.png   lit, not radioed in yet     (the HUD's hollow star)

Copy them into Plugins\LosSantosRED\images\wantedlevel in your game folder and replace LSR's. To get LSR's own back,
copy them in again from LSR's download.

The rest of the setup (LSR's settings and keys) is in the install guide, under "Optional: Los Santos RED".
